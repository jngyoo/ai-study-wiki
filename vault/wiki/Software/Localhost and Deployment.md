# Localhost and Deployment

Localhost is for testing on your own laptop and cannot be shared, whereas deployment makes an application available to users via a public URL.

## Key points

- Localhost is for testing on your own laptop and cannot be shared because the address points to the local machine.
- When a URL is typed, the browser asks for a page, and something sends one back; the difference is who answers (localhost is your laptop talking to itself, hosted sites are machines anyone can reach).
- Frontend is what the browser does with the page it gets back, backend is the machine answering the request, databases are what that machine reads and writes, secrets & auth are how it knows who is asking, and cloud & deployment are how it comes to exist.
- Deploying makes your app available to users with a real URL that can be shared.
